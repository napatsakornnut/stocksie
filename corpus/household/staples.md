# Household standing purchase rules — staples

The household keeps a pre-authorized staples list. The following everyday
grocery categories are standing rules: fresh milk and UHT milk, eggs, rice,
cooking oil, fish sauce, soy sauce, instant noodles, bread, drinking water,
fruit, vegetables, dish soap, laundry detergent, toilet paper, and cleaning
supplies.

A staples purchase is covered by a standing rule when the line total is under
300 baht and the item is on the staples list above. The household buys
staples every week without asking.

Examples of covered staples purchases: two cartons of UHT milk for 94 baht,
a 5-kilogram bag of jasmine rice, a tray of thirty eggs, one bottle of dish
soap, a pack of instant noodles.

A staples purchase over 300 baht is not covered and goes to review.
