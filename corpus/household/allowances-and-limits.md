# Household standing purchase rules — allowances and limits

School supplies for the children — notebooks, pens, pencils, erasers, art
materials, and workbooks — are standing rules when the total is under 500
baht per trip.

Pet food for the family cat is a standing rule under 400 baht per bag.

Household medicine and first-aid supplies — bandages, antiseptic, fever
reducer — are standing rules under 600 baht.

Anything electronics, toys, clothing, games, or gifts is never a standing
rule and always goes to human review, whatever the amount.

Anything above 500 baht total goes to human review whatever the category.

Cash withdrawals, gift cards, and vouchers are never covered by any standing
rule.
